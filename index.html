Mealworm math

Every mealworm video promises a protein farm in a closet, then skips the arithmetic: the 12-week pipeline, the trays of larvae it takes to hit a weekly harvest, and the three-month gap between your starter box and its grandchildren. The colony is arithmetic, not vibes.

Run your numbers

The problem it solves

"Buy 1,000 worms and you're set" is the most repeated sentence in mealworm farming, and it carries no numbers. How many beetles does a 100 g-per-week harvest actually take? How many trays of larvae are sitting in your closet at steady state? When exactly is the first harvest - and why does everything stop for weeks afterwards?

Mealworm math runs the pipeline: your grams-per-week target backwards to beetles, larvae and trays, and your starter box forwards to the capacity it honestly supports. It names the harvest gap - weeks 7 to 20 - before it names you a farmer.

The arithmetic is exact. The biology constants are labeled midpoints of published husbandry guidance; your temperature, feed and luck will argue with them.
